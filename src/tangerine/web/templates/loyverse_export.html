{# Loyverse cost-mirror — upload page (issue #101, parent spec #100). The entry surface of the cost-mirror: a partner uploads a Loyverse back- office items export (the file Loyverse itself recommends for the export→ edit→re-import flow). The prepare step (``/admin/loyverse-export/prepare``) parses it, joins Books' recipe costs, and renders the drift diff; confirm serves the filled round-trip CSV for the partner to upload to Loyverse. Slice 3 (issue #103) adds the drift badge — a quiet line that makes "Loyverse is stale" detectable from inside Books. The badge reads the most recent ``loyverse_exports.confirmed_at`` and the count of cost edits since; it is suppressed before any export (no "stale since forever" message) and counts cost edits only (a recipe edit alone does not inflate it).
